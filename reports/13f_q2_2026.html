Scope. 13F covers long US equity only. Short positions, swaps and non-US listings never appear, and options lines are excluded here because they are not ownership — they are 47% of Citadel’s filing. Filings land 45 days after quarter end, so this is a lagged picture by construction.

Share counts are split-adjusted before differencing. Booking split 25-for-1 and KLA 10-for-1 inside this window; compared as filed, an untouched position would read as adding 2,400%.

An absent position is an exit only when the manager filed. Pershing Square had not filed for Q2 as of 20 August 2026, so its six Q1 positions are reported as unknown rather than as fabricated exits. The other 147 exits are real.

Tickers are resolved by price. 13F carries a CUSIP and an issuer name but never a ticker, so each mapping is accepted only where the price implied by the filers matches the actual quarter-end close. All 148 names resolve at a median error of 0.0000.
